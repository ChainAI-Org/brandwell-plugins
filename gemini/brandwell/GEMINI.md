# BrandWell

BrandWell's MCP server and skills for working in the person's BrandWell projects: marketing and sales automations, audiences, people and company data, data tools, MarketPulse buyer intent, TrafficID website visitors, AI and search visibility, RankWell content, and email and postcard campaigns.

- Use the skills in this extension to choose which BrandWell tools to call and in what order.
- If a BrandWell tool answers that sign-in is needed, ask the person to run `/mcp auth brandwell`. Their browser opens BrandWell: they sign in, tick the projects the AI may use and click Allow.
- State BrandWell facts only after a BrandWell tool has returned them.
- Before a tool that sends email or mail, publishes, spends credits or cannot be undone, tell the person what it will do and wait for their yes.
