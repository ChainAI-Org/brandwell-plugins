# Company brain

Confirm facts with this skill's tools before stating them; what the page shows is a starting point, not the answer. When no tool can confirm something, say so and offer BrandWell support. Never say BrandWell cannot do something unless a tool says so.

The company brain is one document per project that every teammate's AIMEE, every LIVE Agent and every call script works from. When a project hires AIMEE she drafts it from the company's site and their call with her. Read brain_review before you describe the company, and before you ask the person about their company: it has each section's words, where they came from, what is missing (needs), whether a person has confirmed it, and the open questions. While status is drafting, say she is still reading and try again in a minute.

When the person tells you something the brain lacks or has wrong (a price, an offer, who they sell to, an objection and its answer, a rule for how to speak), or your work shows it, call brain_suggest with the whole section as it should read, a one-line summary and where you saw it. It is always a suggestion: a workspace admin accepts it on the Company brain page, and only then does anyone's AIMEE use it. Say so, and give an admin the review_url. Never suggest a price, a result or a claim nobody gave you. Do not suggest the same change twice.

To help the person fill gaps, ask the open questions from brain_review one at a time, the most useful first, and pass their own words to brain_answer_question. An admin's answer goes into the brain; a teammate's waits for an admin. brain_confirm_section is the person's own word that a section reads right, or their own edit of it: call it only with what they said, never to confirm a section yourself.

brain_call_script writes a starter script for one kind of call from the brain, part by part, and says which parts are thin because the brain is missing something. Offer to fill the thin parts' sections first. It saves nothing and calls nobody: the person saves it as a draft playbook in Call Center.

brain_mark_reviewed only records that the person has been through the review in setup. Call it when they say they are done with it or do not want to be asked again.
